Balance traps from the audit

✓ Fixed

💰 Hard zones pay worse

Rewards now scale with the monster. Hard earns about 3x easy at tier 3, so easy zones may now need a look.

✓ Fixed

📉 Most tiers are out of reach

Monsters now grow 1.4x per tier instead of doubling, and existing bosses were rescaled by the migration.

✓ Fixed

🐇 Exploit waiting to spawn

Won fights take at least 2 seconds, and cooldowns carry between fights, so Backstab can't kill Rats in 1 ms.

◐ Partly

✨ Enchanting is weak

Attempts cost 25 essence (down from 100) plus 10,000 gold × level. Each level is still only +2 flat, and it still needs essence of that tier, so the 1,000 cap stays out of reach.

◐ Partly

🪙 Gold has few uses

Enchanting and 100,000-gold raid summons now use gold, but they're small next to hourly income.

◐ Partly

🥊 PvP ignored attack speed

PvP and raids now use a damage figure with the speed compensation removed, so slow weapons don't gain from it. No bouts have been simulated.

🎉 Fun Meter

What players will feel. Only one short hunt has been played in a browser, so most of this still comes from code and simulations.

✓ Fixed

🪦 Rough first hunt

Hunters rest below 50% HP and fight again at 90%, minimum HP went from 30 to 60, and base regen doubled. All five starters finished 30 minutes at tier 1 easy in 25 of 25 runs, though dagger and bow rest a lot.

◐ Partly

Leaderboards, achievements, ability books and rebirth milestones now work. Six achievements won't last long, though.

◐ Untested

Regular hunts still run themselves, so there are few decisions once one starts.

The audit's biggest wins

✓ Fixed

1. Repeated balance scans

Each transaction reads a key at most once. Callers needing many keys read one snapshot document instead of 138 rows.

✓ Fixed

2. One sync per fight

Fights now settle in batches every 30 seconds, and the client replays them. Heartbeats went from every 10 seconds to every 45.

◐ Mostly

3. Every player write refreshing everything

Ownership checks use a separate playerOwners table, so most live queries no longer re-run on every write. The game shell's re-renders weren't addressed.

✓ Fixed

4. History growing forever

A daily cron prunes fight history and task history at 30 days, and loot awards and battle stats at 14.

◐ Mostly

5. Initial download

Initial JS fell from about 985 KB to 667 KB (288 KB to 210 KB gzipped). The 25-query prefetch is gone, and the admin panel loads on its own. The 746 KB image and unminified CSS remain.
